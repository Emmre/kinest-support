# Kinest — Support & Privacy

Official support and privacy pages for **Kinest**, a minimal, offline video player for iPhone and iPad.

Kinest lets you enjoy local videos, organize your folders, manage subtitles, and resume playback without ads or accounts.

## About Kinest

Kinest is designed for a simple, distraction-free video experience.

- Offline playback for compatible MP4, MOV, and M4V files
- Folder-based video browsing
- External SRT subtitle support
- Available audio and subtitle track selection
- Resume playback
- Gesture-based playback controls
- Native iPhone and iPad support

## Privacy

Kinest is designed to work locally on your device.

No account is required, and your videos, imported subtitles, playback history, and preferences are not uploaded to a Kinest server.

For details, read the [Privacy Policy](https://emmre.github.io/kinest-support/privacy.html).
