Kinest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What Kinest does

Kinest is an offline-first video player. It works with local video files that you open through the supported Apple Files workflow.

Information stored on your device

Kinest stores playback history, resume positions, watched status and playback preferences locally on your device. When you import an external subtitle, Kinest keeps a copy in the app's local storage so it remains available after the file picker is dismissed. Imported subtitle copies can be removed from the app.

Information collected by the developer

The current app does not operate developer-controlled accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. No developer-operated collection of your video files, subtitle contents or playback history was identified in the app.

Files providers and device backup

A file selected through Apple Files or a third-party document provider may be subject to that provider's own services and privacy practices. Apple device backup or synchronization may also handle app data according to your device and Apple account settings. These services are separate from developer-operated collection.

Retention and deletion

Local playback preferences and history remain on the device until they are changed, removed by the app, or the app's data is removed through iOS. Removing an imported subtitle from Kinest removes the app-managed copy; it does not modify the original file selected in Files.
