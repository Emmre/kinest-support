Privacy Policy for Kinest

Effective date: October 18, 2026
Last updated: October 18, 2026

Kinest ("Kinest," "we," "us," or "our") is an offline video player for iPhone and iPad, provided by an independent developer based in Türkiye. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Kinest app or contact us for support.

Contact: emreesrc@gmail.com

1. Information handled by the app

Kinest does not require an account and does not send your videos, subtitles, playback history, or preferences to the developer's servers. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

To provide its features, Kinest may access video files and subtitle files that you choose through Apple's Files interface or place in a folder made available to the app. Playback position, playback preferences, and copies of imported subtitle files may be stored locally on your device. These files and settings remain under the control of your device and its operating system. Kinest does not intentionally transmit their contents to us.

If you use iCloud Drive or another file provider through Apple's Files app, that provider may handle the files according to its own terms and privacy practices. Kinest does not control those services.

2. Information you send to us

If you email us for help or with a privacy request, we receive the email address, message, and any other information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot reported issues, and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or private video files unless necessary.

We do not use support messages for targeted advertising, sell personal information, or build advertising profiles.

3. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we may process information you voluntarily provide in support correspondence based on our legitimate interest in answering inquiries, resolving problems, and operating a reliable support channel. Where required by applicable law, we may also process or retain information to comply with a legal obligation. You may contact us to object to processing based on legitimate interests; we will consider requests under applicable law.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or share app content or usage data with advertisers or analytics providers. Support email communications necessarily pass through the email services used by you and us, which process messages under their own privacy and security arrangements. We may disclose information if legally required or when necessary to protect legal rights, subject to applicable law.

5. Retention and deletion

Video files, imported subtitles, playback history, and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you remove them through the app, Files, device settings, or by uninstalling the app, subject to iOS behavior and any backups or third-party file-provider retention.

We aim to delete routine support correspondence within 12 months after the last relevant interaction, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary to resolve an ongoing issue, establish or defend legal claims, or comply with applicable law. This retention commitment must be reflected in our actual email-handling practices.

You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing emreesrc@gmail.com. We will assess and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

6. Security

Kinest relies on iOS app sandboxing and user-directed file access for locally handled content. We take reasonable steps to protect support correspondence, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. You may also have a right to lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority. Contact emreesrc@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify that a request relates to you before responding.

Because Kinest does not maintain user accounts or upload your video library to us, we generally cannot access, retrieve, or delete files and playback information stored only on your device.

8. Children

Kinest is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, please contact us.

9. International use

Kinest may be available in multiple countries. If you contact us, your support correspondence may be processed in Türkiye and through the email providers involved in delivery and storage, potentially across borders, subject to applicable safeguards and law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or our information practices change. The latest version will be posted at https://emmre.github.io/kinest-support/privacy.html with an updated date.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy requests, email emreesrc@gmail.com.
