Kinest Support

Kinest is a local, folder-first video player for iPhone and iPad.

Open a video

Use the Files workflow to make a folder or local video available to Kinest, then open the folder from the library. Kinest focuses on Apple-supported local MP4, MOV and M4V media and the codecs supported by the device.

Import subtitles

Open a video, open Tracks, choose Subtitles, and select Add Subtitle… to import an SRT file. Imported subtitle copies are stored in Kinest so they remain available after the picker closes.

Troubleshooting

  • If a video cannot play, confirm that its container and codec are supported by the device.
  • If a subtitle does not appear, check its timing and encoding, then import it again from Files.
  • For missing folders or files, confirm that the item remains available through the Files provider.
