Getting started

Your videos stay on your device. No account or internet connection required.

01

Add your videos

Use the Files app to place local video files in a folder available to Kinest. For example, use On My iPhone/iPad → Kinest.

02

Open a folder and play

Browse your folders in Kinest, then tap a video to watch. Compatible MP4, MOV, and M4V files play using Apple's native media framework.

Subtitles & audio

Choose available tracks without leaving the player.

  1. Open a video and tap the Subtitles & Audio icon in the player's top controls.
  2. In the compact menu, choose an available subtitle track or select Off.
  3. To use your own file, tap Add Subtitle… and choose an .srt file from Files.
  4. If your video contains multiple audio tracks, select one in the Audio section of the same menu.

Imported subtitle copies are kept locally by Kinest so they remain available after the file picker closes.

Troubleshooting

A few things to check if playback or subtitles don't look right.

My video won't play.

Check that the container and video/audio codecs are supported by your device. A file extension alone does not guarantee compatibility.

My subtitle file isn't showing.

Check the SRT file's timing and text encoding, then try importing it again from Files. Confirm that subtitles are not set to Off.

A folder or video is missing.

Verify that the file or folder is still available in the Files app and that its storage provider has made it accessible locally.

Will Kinest stream videos from the internet?

Kinest focuses on compatible local files. Network streaming and unsupported container formats are not part of its current feature set.
